callbridge far end

Your laptop's mic and speaker as the other side of the call. Joins a room; the phone app joins the same room in room mode.

idle
Sending (your mic → phone)
Receiving (phone → your speaker)
–
Use headphones on the laptop or the phone side will hear itself. Audio is 8 kHz IMA ADPCM in 20 ms frames, the same format the dongle sends; the server relays frames between everyone in the room without touching them.
