callbridge far end

Your laptop's mic and speaker as the other side of the call. Joins a room; the phone app joins the same room in room mode.

Sending (your mic → phone)
Receiving (phone → your speaker)
–
Last 10 s: level sent (blue) and received (orange). The horizontal offset between a burst and its echo is the round trip.
Use headphones on the laptop or the phone side will hear itself. Audio is Opus at 16 kbps in 20 ms frames (40 bytes), the same format the dongle sends over BLE; the server and the phone app relay frames without touching them.
